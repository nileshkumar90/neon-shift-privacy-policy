Privacy Policy

NEON SHIFT — Android · version 1.0.0
✓ NEON SHIFT collects no data of any kind.

There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and nothing leaves your device. You can verify this yourself: the app's permission list contains only optional vibration, and the game runs normally in airplane mode.

What we collect

Nothing.

NEON SHIFT has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no telemetry and no third-party SDKs. It contains no code capable of opening a network connection.

What is stored on your device

Your campaign progress, star ratings, best move counts and accessibility preferences are written to this device's private application storage. Android isolates this storage from other apps.

Uninstalling NEON SHIFT deletes it. You can also erase it at any time without uninstalling, using Reset Progress in the app's Settings screen.

Permissions

  • VIBRATE — used only for the optional haptic feedback that you can switch off in Settings.

No location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or network permission is requested. The app declares no INTERNET permission at all.

Children

NEON SHIFT is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

Third parties

None. No data is shared, sold, rented or exchanged with anyone, because no data is ever collected in the first place. There are no advertising or analytics networks integrated into the app.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights over your personal data — the right to access, correct, export and erase it. Since NEON SHIFT holds none, there is nothing to exercise those rights against. Deleting the app, or using Reset Progress, removes the only data that ever existed.

Security

Your progress never leaves the device, so it is never transmitted over a network and cannot be intercepted. As with any app, keeping your operating system up to date is the main way to protect the device itself.

Changes to this policy

Any future version of NEON SHIFT that changed how data is handled would update this page along with the version number at the top. The promise itself — that the game works entirely offline — is a design constraint of the app, not a setting that can be switched off.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the developer's Google Play Store listing. Because no personal data is collected, any message you send cannot be linked to data this app holds about you.

This page states exactly what the shipped app does. Every claim above is verifiable: check the app's permission list, run it in airplane mode, and search the package for network code. There are none.
